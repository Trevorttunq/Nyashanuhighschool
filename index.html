Academic pathways

Our Academic Departments

Nyashanu High provides a broad curriculum to prepare students for various academic and professional paths.

01

Science Department

Our Science classes encourage curiosity, critical thinking, and problem-solving. Students explore the following subjects:

  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Geography

We focus on practical experiments, analytical skills, and innovation — preparing learners for future careers in medicine, engineering, technology, and other scientific fields.

02

Commercial Department

The Commercial stream helps students develop a strong understanding of business, finance, and economics. Learners study the following subjects:

  • Accounting
  • Economics
  • Business Studies

This prepares students for careers in business, management, entrepreneurship, and finance.

03

Practicals Department

The Practicals Department offers hands-on skills and applied learning. Students explore the following areas:

  • Woodwork
  • Technical Graphics and Design
  • Physical Education, Sports and Mass display
  • Computer Science
  • Agriculture
  • Metalwork
  • Textiles
  • Home Economics
  • Agricultural Practices
  • Computer Applications in Practice

Students develop practical problem-solving skills, technical proficiency, and creativity — preparing them for vocational careers and applied sciences.

04

Arts Department

Our Arts classes nurture creativity, communication, and cultural awareness. Students develop their talents through:

  • Literature in English
  • History
  • Heritage
  • Shona
  • Family and Religious Studies

We emphasize self-expression, critical thinking, and social understanding — equipping learners for future paths in law, media, education, design, and the creative industries.
