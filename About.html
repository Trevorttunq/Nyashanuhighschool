About Nyashanu High School

A mission school in Buhera built around education, faith, community and the development of young people.

Who we are

Education With Purpose

Nyashanu High School is a Reformed Church in Zimbabwe school in Buhera District, Manicaland. The school is part of the wider Nyashanu Mission, which also includes a church, primary school and clinic. The mission was established in 1954.

Nyashanu Mission • Buhera, Manicaland, Zimbabwe
Our Mission

Growing Through Education, Faith & Service

Nyashanu High School's stated vision is to be a collective Ubuntu repository of synergies of education excellence with inclusivity and integrity by 2030.

Its mission focuses on the transformation of human dignity through connectivism and accountability, reflecting the school's emphasis on education as a way of serving learners and the wider community.

The school is known for combining academic development with agriculture, sport, practical learning, community service and technology.
